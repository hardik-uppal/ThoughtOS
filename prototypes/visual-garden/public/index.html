A desk, not a puzzle.

  1. Open a card. Use Edit note to write. Tags opens the same editor at the tag field.
  2. Connect two thoughts. Click Connect on a card, search for another, then choose Connect. Both cards show the link. Or write a new, connected thought.
  3. Follow any branch. Explore chain puts a thought between its connections. Click any neighbouring card to continue. Back, Forward and the breadcrumb trail keep your place.
  4. Tags are not links. Nearby thoughts share tags but are labelled not linked. Only you decide to connect them.
