Make room for your thoughts

When enabled, note text and searches go to this desk's local Ollama model. Nothing is sent to a cloud service. Requires the server's local-model option. Without it, text and tags still work.

Off by default. No keystroke recording. Keeps the latest 20 completed-action snapshots in this browser. Named snapshots are kept until you delete them.

A place to come back to

Snapshots include notes, connections and desk settings. They belong to this browser and address. Restore first saves a safety copy; open that copy to reverse a restore. Clearing browser data removes snapshots.

Why these thoughts connect

A desk, not a puzzle.

  1. Open a card. Use Edit note to write. Tags opens the same editor at the tag field.
  2. Connect two thoughts. Click Connect on a card, search for another, then choose Connect. Both cards show the link. Or write a new, connected thought.
  3. Follow any branch. Explore keeps the current thought beside connected and unconnected notes. Click any neighbouring card to continue. Back, Forward and the breadcrumb trail keep your place.
  4. Tags are not links. Unconnected notes stay available, even when no tags match. Only you decide to connect them.
